## 📖 Overview

**Wuchang Save Architect** is a Windows-native companion utility designed for players of *Wuchang: Fallen Feathers* who want to understand, shape, and refine their character's progression across the sprawling, mist-laden world that the game presents. Rather than treating your save as a static artifact, this project treats it as a living blueprint — something you can study, annotate, and gently re-sculpt as your understanding of the game deepens.

Where a blunt instrument might simply toggle numbers, Save Architect takes the stance of a **restorer of paintings**: careful, layered, respectful of the original. The result is a tool that feels less like a switchboard and more like a quiet workshop.

The 2026 edition consolidates years of community feedback, brings a refreshed responsive interface, expands multilingual coverage, and extends the companion support rotation so that someone is always available to help — at any hour, in any timezone.

## 🎨 The Philosophy Behind Wuchang Save Architect

The name is intentional. An *architect* does not demolish a building to fix a leaky roof — they study the structure, understand the load-bearing walls, and then apply the smallest possible intervention that solves the problem. That is precisely the disposition this project adopts toward your save data.

Three principles guided every decision:

1. **Minimal Intervention, Maximum Clarity.** Every feature exists to make the state of your character *legible*, not to overwhelm you with toggles you will never use.
2. **Reversibility as a Virtue.** Before any change is committed, a snapshot is preserved. You can always rewind the clock.
3. **Local-First Ethics.** Your save data never needs to leave your machine. Everything happens on your disk, under your control.

These principles echo the game's own meditation on memory, loss, and restoration — but applied to the humble domain of a save file.

## ✨ Feature Constellation

Below is a constellation of what you will find inside. Each entry is grouped by theme rather than importance, because in a workshop, no tool is truly "minor."

### 🛠️ Core Capabilities

- **Save Snapshot & Rollback System** — Before any adjustment, a timestamped snapshot is created. Roll back at any moment with a single confirmation.
- **Stat Sculpting Console** — Adjust character statistics with clear visuals showing the ripple effects across derived values.
- **Inventory Cartography** — A visual map of your inventory, with sorting, filtering, and category grouping to help you plan rather than hoard.
- **Progression Ledger** — A chronological record of decisions you have applied, with notes you can attach to each entry.
- **Build Preset Vault** — Save build configurations and switch between them as you experiment with different playstyles.
- **Weather & Time Insight Panel** — View the in-game clock and weather context for planning your excursions (read-only, informational).
- **Boss Encounter Journal** — Track which challenges you have faced and which remain, purely as a reference ledger.
- **Fast Travel Point Index** — A catalogue of discovered travel nodes for quick reference.

### 🎨 Interface & Experience

- **Responsive UI** — The layout adapts gracefully from a 1366×768 laptop to a 4K ultrawide, without awkward stretching or hidden panels.
- **Dark and Light Themes** — A palette that respects long sessions in dim rooms, alongside a crisp daytime mode.
- **Keyboard-First Navigation** — Every critical action is reachable without a mouse, for those who prefer the old ways.
- **Adjustable Density** — Compact mode for power users and a relaxed layout for those who prefer breathing room.
- **Accessibility Touches** — High-contrast option, scalable font tiers, and colour-blind-friendly indicator shapes.

### 🌍 Language & Community

- **Multilingual Interface** — Ships with English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), and Russian localisations out of the box.
- **Community Translation Channel** — Localisation strings are stored externally in plain files, so contributors can refine phrasing without touching any code.
- **24/7 Customer Support** — A rotating support rota means questions are typically answered within a few hours, regardless of your timezone.
- **In-App Guided Tours** — Small, contextual walkthroughs introduce each panel the first time you open it.

### 🔒 Safety & Integrity

- **Read-Before-Write Verification** — Every write operation is preceded by a checksum verification of the current file state.
- **Atomic Save Writes** — Changes are written to a temporary file and swapped in, preventing partial writes that could corrupt data.
- **No External Telemetry** — Nothing is phoned home. Ever. The tool is silent about your existence, by design.
- **Explicit Consent Prompts** — No silent changes; every meaningful action asks for confirmation.

### ⚙️ Under the Hood

- **Deterministic Behaviour** — The same input state always produces the same output, which makes troubleshooting predictable.
- **Modular Panel Architecture** — Each UI panel is isolated, so a failure in one does not cascade into others.
- **Extensive Logging** — Human-readable logs in plain text for those who like to inspect the gears.
- **Config Portability** — Your preferences travel with you via a single portable configuration file.

## 🤔 Why a Save Architect Instead of a Generic Trainer?

The word "trainer" has, over the years, accumulated a certain connotation — a blunt row of toggles, a scattergun of switches, a vending machine of instant outcomes. This project deliberately charts a different course.

Consider the difference between a **vending machine** and a **coffee roastery**. Both, ultimately, deliver coffee. But one hands you a paper cup and asks nothing of you, while the other invites you to understand the bean, the roast, the grind. Save Architect is the roastery: it slows you down just enough to make the outcome *yours*.

This is why the project emphasizes snapshots, ledgers, and reversibility. It is not about removing the journey — it is about letting you re-read the journey with a sharper eye.

## 🚀 Getting Started — The Gentle Path

The onboarding is designed to feel like being handed a map rather than being thrown into a labyrinth.

1. **Acquire the Companion Package.** Use the distribution channel provided by the maintainers. The package is a single portable archive, no installer wizardry required.
2. **Extract to a Quiet Folder.** Place it somewhere like `D:\Tools\WuchangSaveArchitect` — avoid system directories and cloud-synced folders, which can introduce file-locking quirks.
3. **Launch the Application.** Double-click the main executable. On first run, you will be greeted by a short welcome panel explaining the layout.
4. **Point the Tool at Your Save Directory.** The application will offer to detect the default location; if it guesses wrong, you can browse manually.
5. **Create Your First Snapshot.** Before doing anything else, press the snapshot button. This is your safety net.
6. **Explore at Your Own Pace.** Open panels one at a time. Each has a small "?" icon that explains its purpose in plain language.
7. **Configure Preferences.** Choose your language, theme, and density. These settings persist between sessions.
8. **Join the Support Channel.** Even if you have no questions, lurking is welcome — the community shares tips and build ideas regularly.

That is the entire ritual. No command-line incantations, no dependency rituals, no mysterious scripts.

## 🖥️ The Responsive Interface, Explained

Responsiveness in this project is not merely about resizing gracefully. It is about **respecting your context**.

- On a **single 13-inch laptop**, panels stack vertically and the sidebar collapses into an icon rail.
- On a **dual-monitor developer setup**, you can detach the Inventory Cartography panel into its own window and keep it permanently visible.
- On an **ultrawide**, the layout expands horizontally with generous whitespace, so your eyes are not forced to travel unnaturally.
- On a **tablet running Windows in portrait**, the interface prioritises vertical reading flow, placing the most-used controls within thumb reach.

Every layout decision was ruthlessly interrogated: does this help the user see more, or does it merely fill space? If the answer was the latter, the element was cut.

## 🌐 Multilingual Soul of the Tool

A tool that speaks only one language whispers to a fraction of the world. Save Architect aims to *speak*, not whisper.

All localisation content lives in human-readable resource files. Adding a new language does not require touching the core application. A contributor can fork the repository, add a language folder, mirror the structure of an existing language, and submit a pull request. The maintainers review localisation pull requests with particular care, because language is not just translation — it is tone, rhythm, and hospitality.

If your language is not yet represented, you are warmly invited to open an issue titled `New Language Proposal: <Your Language>` and the maintainers will coordinate with you.

Support covers:

Support does **not** cover requests to alter the game's protected systems, distribute modified binaries, or bypass the game's authentication. Please respect this boundary — it exists to keep the project alive and useful for the long haul.

## 🗡️ Build Archetypes You Can Sculpt

Because Save Architect encourages reflection, players often use it to *study* their builds rather than merely adjust them. Some archetypes the community has been exploring in 2026:

- **The Wandering Cartographer** — a build optimised for exploration, with heavy investment in traversal and discovery stats.
- **The Featherweight Duelist** — a nimble, low-armour configuration prioritising quick reactions.
- **The Ashen Chronicle-Keeper** — a scholar-style build focused on lore and item discovery.
- **The Silent Ferryman** — a defensive build designed for long, patient encounters.
- **The Foghorn** — an unconventional high-noise, high-presence build that thrives on disruption.

Each of these can be saved as a preset in the vault and swapped between sessions, letting you A/B test your ideas without rebuilding from scratch each time.
